Python is the dominant language for AI and data science due to its simplicity and rich ecosystem.

Core libraries:
- NumPy: numerical computing and array operations
- Pandas: data manipulation and analysis
- scikit-learn: classical machine learning algorithms
- TensorFlow / PyTorch: deep learning frameworks
- LangChain: building applications with large language models

Python's readability and the availability of pre-built AI libraries make it the go-to choice for both research and production AI systems.
